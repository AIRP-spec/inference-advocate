# Generated evaluator-training artifacts

Paper: step 8.

This directory holds outputs of `tools/evaluator-training/`. The recipe and
prompts are in that tools directory. Corpus JSONL and the review sample are
produced by a generator run, not committed from an empty tree. LoRA adapters,
merged weights, GGUF files, and gate reports land under `artifacts/` and are
gitignored.

Do not copy held-out items into this directory by hand.
